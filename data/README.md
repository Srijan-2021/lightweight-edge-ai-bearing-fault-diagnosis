# Data directory

This directory is created locally. Raw vibration recordings and generated feature tables are intentionally excluded from Git.

Recommended layout:

```text
data/
├── raw/
│   ├── K001/
│   │   └── *.mat
│   ├── K002/
│   │   └── *.mat
│   └── cwru_48k_de/
│       └── *.mat
└── processed/
    ├── paderborn_vibration_features.csv
    └── ...
```

- Place the original Paderborn bearing folders under `data/raw/`.
- Place the selected CWRU 48 kHz drive-end MATLAB recordings under `data/raw/cwru_48k_de/` for the supplementary study.
- Processed CSVs are generated by the notebooks and remain local unless a small curated result is deliberately added under `results/`.
- Do not upload or commit raw recordings unless their original data-provider terms explicitly permit redistribution.

See the Paderborn University Bearing DataCenter and Case Western Reserve University Bearing Data Center for the original datasets; consult their current terms before use.
